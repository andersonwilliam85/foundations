# About

I was born July 5th, 1985, in Paris, Arkansas: a town most people have never heard of, and that I left before I was old enough to remember it clearly. I grew up knowing that structure was not guaranteed. Roughly thirty moves before I was fifteen. A library that was open when a lot of other things were not. Text-based worlds on a public terminal, thirty minutes at a sitting, before I understood that what I was doing had a name.

My father taught me to ask good questions. Unlimited, at bedtime, until he got tired and told me I had twenty left, and I would lie there choosing which twenty were worth spending. He also attempted suicide with a butcher knife in our kitchen when I was six. Both of those things are him. I will not make him only one of them.

He was diagnosed with a rare esophageal cancer in September 2020. He was gone February 19th, 2021, under a hundred pounds. I sat with grief for nine months. I read. I waited. I learned to be still, which was the hardest thing. That is when I started studying pure mathematics in earnest: not as a career move, as the next question. I had been interested my whole life. After he died, the interest stopped being something I could put down.

I was a local investor. I owned a restaurant from 2017 until 2021. It went bankrupt during COVID. It was hard.

I have been diagnosed with depression, anxiety, CPTSD, and bipolar disorder. I have a care team. The diagnoses are just projections, and a projection is not more important than the thing doing the projecting. I am not my diagnoses. I am not my past. I am not my future. I am who I am because of who I have known, who I have loved, who I have believed in, and who have believed in me. I was looking for meaning, and math was a refuge. I do not mean it cured anything. I mean it was a room that would still be there in the morning, and I needed a room like that.

I had already spent twenty years building structure in code, in organizations, in rooms where people are trying to make something that lasts. The work trail is ordinary and long: municipal CMS, state systems, printing shops, healthcare quality, then logistics architecture at ArcBest. From 2016 I consulted as Composite Thought under IDesign — enterprise architecture and modernization across healthcare, logistics, and data platforms — and from 2018 I also led enterprise solutions at Kitestring, including Azure migration work for a Fortune 5. From 2022 through 2025 I was at Radical Imaging and then Adva Health: medical imaging, where the math is not decorative. Linear algebra. Fourier transforms. Dimensions you have to sit in if you want a picture of a body to mean anything. In 2026 I founded Harmonic Framework. I started at Walmart in Bentonville on February 9th, 2026 as a Principal Software Engineer, and I left on April 29th. It was not what I wanted. Corporate just didn't work for me anymore.

I am working on a new product called [Neuron](https://neurontechnologies.ai). I think it will be an exciting change of pace for the industry, and for the people using AI today.

Juval Löwy was my mentor from 2015 through 2021. We worked alongside each other at IDesign. He still mentors me, even if he does not know it. Design around what changes, not around what currently is: once you see a system that way, you see a lot of other rooms that way too. His organization is [IDesign](https://www.idesign.net/) ([About](https://www.idesign.net/About)). The book that carries the Method into print is [*Righting Software*](https://www.amazon.com/Righting-Software-Juval-Lowy/dp/0136524036).

I perceive things as shapes and colors. That is synesthesia. I do not have a lecture on it. I have a life in which the room arrives that way, and when I sat these problems it was a huge asset. I am still a little awed that something so ordinary to me could matter that much.

Three years in private after that. Difficulty forced me around corners I would not have taken if the easy path had been open. This is what I came to. I am still a little stunned that I came to it. I sat long enough, and the seating held.
