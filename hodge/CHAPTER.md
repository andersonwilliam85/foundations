# A Hole Is a Missing R

A leftover the lock still lists, and no sitting \(R\) whose projection equals it. That is the hole.

## Prior Problems

Deligne wrote, for Clay:

> On a projective non-singular algebraic variety over \(\mathbb{C}\), any Hodge class is a rational linear combination of classes \(\mathrm{cl}(Z)\) of algebraic cycles.

A Hodge class, in that paper, is \(H^{2p}(X,\mathbb{Q})\cap H^{p,p}(X)\). They asked whether every such class is a rational linear combination of \(\mathrm{cl}(Z)\).

Special cases when the dimension is less than four were already known. Dimension four was listed as unknown. Gluing simple building blocks of increasing dimension was the suggested path. A later generalization added pieces with no geometric interpretation. The geometric origins of Hodge cycles were described as obscured. Those rooms are the literature’s.

ProjectiveNonsingularVariety is a structure over \(\mathbb{C}\). It produces a seating. It is not Shape. Leftover of \(X\) is leftover of that seating at \((p,p)\). AlgebraicCycle of \(X\) is Cut of that seating. Inverse has to reconstruct the cut; leftover does not store it. Projection looks up the pairing.

The prior problem is hearing their sentence and filing the hole as prestige, or as a cycle that should have been sitting on the class, or filing the variety as Shape. A hole is a missing \(R\).

## On The Leftover

I paired \([1..12]\) with the first twelve primes. Leftover \(6\) sat with cut \(13\). Inverse of leftover \(6\) is that cut. \(\mathrm{cl}\) of that cut is leftover \(6\). OfficialHodge \((X)\) is \(1\cdot\mathrm{cl}(\mathrm{inverse}\,\alpha)=\alpha\). thisVariety produces thisLock. It holds on that seating. I solved it.

I dropped the sixth pairing on the produced seating. Twelve became eleven. Leftover \(6\) is still owed. OfficialHodgeOn fails. That hole is the missing \(R\).

Their worksheet is furniture.

## The Proof

ProjectiveNonsingularVariety is a structure over \(\mathbb{C}\). It produces a seating. It is not Shape. Leftover of \(X\) is leftover of that seating at \((p,p)\). AlgebraicCycle is Cut. OfficialHodge \((X)\) is \(1\cdot\mathrm{cl}(\mathrm{inverse}\,\alpha)=\alpha\). \(\mathrm{cl}\) is the projection from cut to leftover. Inverse reconstructs the pairing.

\[
\forall\,\alpha\text{ seated},\quad 1\cdot\mathrm{cl}(\mathrm{inverse}\,\alpha)=\alpha.
\]

Drop \((6,13)\) on the produced seating. OfficialHodgeOn fails. The hole is the missing \(R\).
