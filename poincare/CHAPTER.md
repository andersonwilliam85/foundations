# A Complete Meeting Is the Sphere, Not the World

Poincaré asked whether a space with no hole, and closed, must be a 3-sphere. Ricci flow is the path Perelman used. I sit the meeting.

I have wanted complete things since I was a child who did not have them. A library that stayed open. A father who brought the same turtle back every night and then could not hold the rest of the structure. Completeness was the hunger. It was never permission to file the thing I could hold as the sky.

## Prior Problems

A hole is a missing relationship. If nothing is missing, the meeting takes the whole lock. Closed is the meeting finishing.

The prior problem is hearing complete and filing the ball as the sky. Completeness is nothing missing from the relationship, and the meeting finishing. The shape you can hold is not ontology. Things have shapes; we do not live in a soup. A dent comes out round. That does not make the dent the sky.

I have seen this hunger in architecture. A system comes out clean and somebody files the cleanliness as the business. A scan comes out round and somebody files the roundness as the patient. Same hunger. Different room.

In 1904 Poincaré asked whether the 3-sphere is the unique simply connected 3-manifold. Simply connected means every owed loop sits: no hole. The unique working shape of a complete meeting is the dent. The dent is not the world.

The literature filed Poincaré as a special case of Thurston geometrization: eight geometries, standard pieces, a catalog you can sort a space into. Join of finished meetings is still a meeting. Completeness does not become a catalog of worlds.

Perelman proved every 3-manifold is built from those pieces. Ricci flow, surgery, Hamilton’s program, Cheeger–Gromov collapsing: that is the path he walked. That work stands. This page seats the meeting. \(S^3\) is still not the world.

The community teaches simply connected with a rubber band. On an apple it shrinks. On a doughnut it does not. The apple is a closed meeting: the dent sat the lock and nothing is missing. The doughnut is a hole: a seat missing. Both are theorems. I did not need a flow to tell an apple from a doughnut.

The 2-sphere was already characterized by simple connectivity. A smaller lock, two seats, closed, is complete. I seat that known case. I do not pretend the apple was the hard room.

The question kept for the 3-sphere is the one I sat with twelve seats. A complete twelve-seat meeting is the dent. It is not the world. The lock stays twelve. I do not shrink it to make the sitting look cheaper, and I do not grow it to make the sitting look like a cosmos.

Ricci flow is Perelman's path. The chapter’s cut is Lean now: Ricci is not the object of this seating, in the same voice that says \(S^3\) is not the world.

Geometrization is a catalog of pieces. Completeness is the meeting finishing.

Closed, in the official language, means compact, no tearing, stay on the surface. Dent equals lock and nothing missing. Tearing is a missing seat. Leaving the surface is the dent missing the lock. I sit those names on the meeting I already had.

Clay lists this problem Solved. Perelman, 2010. That filing is true. I still seat the meeting on this page.

## On The Dent

I pressed twelve seats. The dent sat the lock. Whole. Closed. That ball is a working shape: a dent you can hold. I dropped one seat. Eleven relationships. The dent missed the lock. I did not name that meeting whole.

A complete lock is whole. A missing tooth is not. Ricci is the path. \(S^3\) is not the world.

The sphere, here, is the working shape of a complete meeting. Poincaré, here, is that seating, held in Lean. The written proof is [PROOF.md](PROOF.md).

People hear complete and want a cosmos. I understand the temptation. I sat long enough to want one myself. Wanting a universe from a round dent is the same appetite that wants a prize from a pour. The dent is still a dent. You can hold it. You can work in it. You do not get to move in and call it the sky.

The meeting finished. The sphere is the dent. It is not the world.
