## Prior Problems

> $`\operatorname{rank}\,E(\mathbb{Q})=\operatorname{ord}_{s=1}L(E,s)`$.

They asked whether the rank of $E(\mathbb{Q})$ equals the order of vanishing of $L(E,s)$ at $s=1$.
