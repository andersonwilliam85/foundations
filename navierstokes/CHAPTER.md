# A Blow-Up Is a Hole in an Instant

Fefferman’s official statement writes velocity and pressure as fields of place and time, then a force, then a viscosity, then an energy, then four doors, and calls that Navier–Stokes.

A scan is a sequence. One slice can sit and the next can lie. I learned that in a room where a picture of a body is only honest if every seat you owe is still sitting when the next instant arrives. A flow is that honesty stretched across time. Smooth means every instant holds. Blow-up means one of them does not.

## Prior Problems

Fefferman wrote the official statement for Clay. I sit with that statement.

It asks for a velocity field and a pressure field, functions of place and of time. Velocity is the dent. Pressure is the lock. Two readings of one meeting.

It asks that the flow be incompressible: the divergence sits at zero. The dent owes exactly the lock.

It asks for an initial condition at time zero. That is the first instant.

It asks for a force \(f\). An outside pairing. Doors A and B take none. Doors C and D allow one.

It asks for a viscosity \(\nu > 0\), and is careful that Euler \(\nu = 0\) is not this prize. Euler is not this prize. I sit the flow without making viscosity the object.

It asks for decay at infinity so the whole-space lock behaves. A cycle the shape never owed is not a hole.

It asks for \(C^\infty\). Every instant Whole, and no missing seat.

It asks for bounded energy. When every piece stays whole, the bound stays whole.

It asks for two settings, whole space and periodic. Two locks, open and periodic. A flow is not both.

Then four statements. A: every unforced open-lock flow stays smooth. B: every unforced periodic-lock flow stays smooth. C: there exists a forced open-lock flow that breaks. D: there exists a forced periodic-lock flow that breaks. Clay asks for one of those four. I sat all four.

The plane is already known. Three dimensions are the lock. Twelve belonging pairings stay.

The literature knows a solution can exist for a little while, and that if the maximal time is finite something has blown up. A hole before the meeting finishes.

A small enough start stays smooth. A small Whole lock stays Whole if you do not drop it.

The prior problem is hearing those doors and filing the equation as the object. The seating is the flow.

## On the Instant

A flow owes a lock-shape. Each instant is a shape. If an instant owes a pairing, that pairing has to sit or the sequence has already blown.

Smooth means every instant is whole: every owed cycle is sitting. Blow-up means some instant has a hole. I dropped \(\langle 6, 13 \rangle\) from twelve pairings. Eleven relationships. That instant holed. Call it smooth and the seating is a lie.

Join two whole instants and the join is whole. That is the bound. Join a whole thing to a hole and the hole is still there. The bound does not save you from a piece you punched.

A finished flow is a meeting whose every instant sat. That meeting is complete. A hole in an instant is a hole. You can hold it. You do not get to sell it as the weather.

People hear blow-up and want a disaster, or a prize. I understand the temptation. A broken instant is still a missing relationship in a sequence. Nothing more glamorous than that.
